Our Story

2021 · Pushpa Vatika

The first glance

In the Ramayana, Ram first saw Sita in the flower garden of Mithila. Ours was a marigold-filled garden party in Jaipur — one shy smile across the jasmine, and the world quietly rearranged itself.

2024 · Shiva Dhanush

The promise

No mighty bow was strung, but a promise was. On the steps of an old stepwell at dawn, Ram asked, and Sita said yes before he could finish the question.

2025 · Swayamvar

Two families, one heart

Ayodhya met Mithila over chai, kachoris and a great deal of laughter. The elders consulted the stars, the stars agreed, and the date was set.

2027 · Saptapadi

Seven steps, forever

सखा सप्तपदा भव

“With these seven steps, become my friend.” Around the sacred fire in the Pink City, we take our seven vows — and we would love you there.

Travel & Stay

By Air

Jaipur International Airport (JAI) is about 25 minutes from the palace. Our welcome desk will greet arriving guests on 24–27 March.

By Rail

Jaipur Junction (JP) is roughly 15 minutes away. Shatabdi and Vande Bharat trains connect from Delhi in about 4½ hours.

By Road

About 5 hours from Delhi on the Delhi–Jaipur expressway. Shuttles will run between the guest hotels and every event.

Where to Stay

Rooms are held for our guests at the palace and two nearby hotels. Share your dates with the hospitality desk and we’ll arrange the rest.

Weather

Late March is warm by day (around 32°C) and pleasant at night (around 18°C). Light cottons for daytime, a shawl for the evenings.

Explore Jaipur

Amer Fort at sunrise, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar and the bangle lanes of Johari Bazaar — ask us for a guided morning.
